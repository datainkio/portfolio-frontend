These are the project's own classes for decoration, and each page shows the
syntax and a live sample. They sit between the tokens and the components:
they combine tokens into one reusable effect and own no markup. The ones
defined with `@utility` in `styles/` take responsive and state variants, such
as `md:inner-border`. The full list is in `reports/design-system/inventory.md`.

- **[Gels](background/gels/)**: translucent colour plates.
- **[Geometric patterns](background/geometric-patterns/)**: halftone,
  checkerboard, and stripes.
- **[Graph paper](background/graph-paper/)**: an engineering grid.
- **[Radius, shadow, and border](radius-shadow-and-border/)**: corner radii,
  the shadow set, inner borders, and masks.
- **[Registration marks](registration-marks/)**: corner crop marks.
- **[Arrows and text](arrows-and-text/)**: arrows before and after a label,
  and one word per line.
